# Portfolio Analytics

Private dashboard:

https://manvinder-portfolio.goatcounter.com

## What to watch

1. **Visitors**: the clearest top-level measure of portfolio reach.
2. **Referrers and campaigns**: where useful traffic came from.
3. **Resume clicks**: stronger recruiter intent than a page view.
4. **Project repository clicks**: which work creates real curiosity.
5. **Writing, X, and contact clicks**: what people want to explore next.

## Event names

- `resume-hero` and `resume-section`
- `project-*-repository` and `project-*-preview`
- `writing-*`
- `meme-*` and `x-profile-*`
- `contact-email`, `contact-linkedin`, `contact-x`, and `contact-github`

GoatCounter filters localhost, common bots, prerender requests, frames, and rapid duplicate session events. Ad blockers can still suppress some browser-based visits, so totals should be treated as directional rather than a perfect census.
