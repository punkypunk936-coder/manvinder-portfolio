01 / Attention & contentDaily workflow

Meme Scout Agent

An always-on editorial desk that watches high-signal AI and prediction-market accounts, rejects posts without a real angle, develops one timely meme and sends it to Telegram. After I publish, it tracks engagement and feeds the result back into future selection.

Why it mattersIt shortens reaction time without turning every tweet into content. Selection, taste, delivery and learning live in one loop.

02 / Writing & distributionDaily workflow

X-to-Substack Pipeline

A private writing desk that turns my long-form X articles into reviewable Substack drafts, keeps formatting and media intact, and separates published work from drafts that still need attention.

Why it mattersIt removes the repeated copying, reformatting, image handling and draft cleanup between two places where I publish.

03 / Trading intelligenceActive use

Crypto Trading Agent

A decision-support layer for my own trading. It ranks setups, records why a position exists, monitors whether the thesis is still intact, explains realized and unrealized PnL, and makes post-trade review harder to avoid.

Why it mattersIt turns market calls into logged decisions and gives me a more honest record of entries, exits, missed moves and changing conviction.
